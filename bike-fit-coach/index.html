Your sessions on this phone

    Show the setup steps again

    Before you start

    • Choose how your bike will stay up, above. The steps here change to match.
    • Level the bike first. The middle of the front wheel should be as high off the floor as the middle of the back wheel. A trainer that holds the back wheel lifts it, so if the front sits lower, put a riser block or a thick book under the front wheel.
    • On rollers, set up with a wall on the far side, away from the phone, so you can hold on while you get on, hold still, and start and stop pedalling.
    • A wall on the far side of the bike, away from the phone. Lean the bike only a little, with the bars straight.
    • Squeeze the front brake gently to keep the bike still. Only the hand nearest the phone needs to be on top of the brake lever (the hood).
    • Your friend stands up straight on the far side, away from the phone, next to the saddle, and holds the bike still. Where they hold it is up to them, as long as they do not crouch.
    • Turn the volume up and switch off silent mode. Everything is spoken.
    • When you tap Start, an iPhone asks twice: for the camera and for motion. Tap Allow both times. Motion is how it knows the phone is level.
    • Rotation lock off. Open Control Centre (swipe down from the top-right corner, or up from the bottom on an iPhone with a Home button) and check the lock with the circle arrow is not lit.
    • About 3 m of space beside the bike, and something 60 to 80 cm high for the phone, such as a chair with a box on it.
    • Light on you, not a window behind you.
    • Normal riding kit and shoes.

    What it checks

    • Your saddle height first, then your reach and bar height, on a road bike with drop handlebars.
    • A few minutes.
    • You need: a trainer or rollers, a wall or a friend to keep the bike up, something 60 to 80 cm high for the phone, and 3 m of space.
    • You hold the pedal at its lowest point for 3 seconds, then pedal: forwards for 30 seconds on a trainer or rollers, backwards for 20 seconds against a wall or with a friend holding the bike.
    • No video is kept unless you switch it on above.

    Add this to your Home Screen: tap Share, then Add to Home Screen. In Safari, your past sessions can be deleted if you do not open this page for 7 days.
