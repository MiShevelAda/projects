Before you start

  • Turn the volume up and switch off silent mode. Everything is spoken.
  • When you tap Start, an iPhone asks twice: for the camera and for motion. Tap Allow both times. Motion is how it knows the phone is level.
  • Rotation lock off. Open Control Centre (swipe down from the top-right corner) and check the lock with the circle arrow is not lit.
  • Bike almost upright, with its far side lightly against a wall. Or a helper on the far side, hidden behind you.
  • About 3 m of space beside the bike, and something 60 to 80 cm high for the phone, such as a chair with a box on it.
  • Light on you, not a window behind you.
  • Normal riding kit and shoes. No trainer needed.

Add this to your Home Screen: tap Share, then Add to Home Screen. In Safari, your past sessions can be deleted if you do not open this page for 7 days.

Your sessions on this phone

    What it checks

    • Your saddle height first, then your reach and bar height.
    • About 3 minutes.
    • You need: a wall or a helper, something 60 to 80 cm high for the phone, and 3 m of space.
    • You hold the pedal at the bottom for 3 seconds, then pedal backwards for 20 seconds.
    • No video is kept unless you switch it on above.

    Private by design

    Everything runs on this phone. No video is kept unless you switch it on above, and nothing is uploaded. Your results stay in this browser.
