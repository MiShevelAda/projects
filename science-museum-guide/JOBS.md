# What First Walk is hired to do

Jobs to Be Done, for a first-time visitor to the Science Museum. Each feature in the app
exists because of one of these jobs; anything that serves none of them stays out.

## The main job

> **When** I am in London with a few hours and have never been to the Science Museum,
> **help me** see the best of it in the time I have, understand what I am looking at, and never feel lost,
> **so that** I leave feeling I really saw it, not that I wandered past it.

## The jobs along the way, and what does each one

| When I... | I want to... | So the app has |
|---|---|---|
| decide whether to go now | know it is open, and until when | **Today** card: Open Now, closes at 18:00, last entry 17:15, Book a Free Ticket |
| have a fixed amount of time | see the best things that fit, in an order that does not double back | **How Long Have You Got?** 1, 2 or 3 hours, each a walk of its own |
| am walking to the next thing | be told the way, and use the walk to learn something | **The way there**: spoken directions, then **On the way**, a short piece that needs no looking |
| think I have arrived | know I am in the right place | **You are there when...**, one visual cue per stop, and a floor picture |
| stand in front of it | know where to look and why it matters, and enjoy it | **The story**: witty, with nudges ("look up, further, there"), read along on screen, tap a paragraph to replay it |
| want to make sure I see it properly | have a short list of things to find | **Look For**, ticked off one by one |
| find a gallery packed | come back to it without losing my place | **Too Busy? Do This Later**: the stop moves to the end of the walk |
| am running out of time | know before it is too late, and what to drop | **The time check**: what is left against when the galleries start clearing, and a shorter walk that fits |
| have no signal deep inside a gallery | carry on regardless | **Save the Walk for Offline**, and the app on the Home Screen |
| need the basics | find coats, food, step-free routes, entrances | **Visit** tab |
| want to keep my phone in my pocket | listen hands-free | the lock-screen and headphone controls; the story waits for play, the way there plays by itself |

## Decided, and why

- **Audio first, screen second.** Visitors look at the museum, not the phone. Everything the
  screen says, the voice says; the screen is there to glance at.
- **Three walks by time, not by theme.** Time is the constraint every first-timer has; theme is a
  second-visit question.
- **The voice is one voice** (his ruling: "Just chose one voice mvp is fine").
- **No map.** A wrong map is worse than none, and the museum's own signs are good; the app gives
  the floor, the zone colour and a landmark instead.
