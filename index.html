Bike Fit Coach

Checks your saddle height and riding position through the phone camera. It talks you through every step, so you can stay on the bike.

Before you start

  • Turn the volume up and switch off silent mode. Everything is spoken.
  • Bike almost upright, with its far side lightly against a wall. Or a helper on the far side, hidden behind you.
  • About 3 m of space beside the bike, and a chair or box for the phone.
  • Normal riding kit and shoes. No trainer needed.
