# First Walk, version 2: the plan

How Apple's own product and engineering teams would build v2: decide what the visitor is trying to
get done (JOBS.md), make the one obvious action on each screen do it, keep the phone in the pocket,
and never let a clever feature break the basic walk. Everything here is for v2 only; v1 stays live
until v2 is finished and tested (his ruling: "keep always a functional version online").

## How they would think

- **Audio first, glance second.** The visitor is looking at a whale, not a phone. Every screen is
  readable in two seconds; everything said is also written.
- **One primary action per screen**, at the thumb: Start, Play, Next, Ask.
- **Hide the waiting.** Recordings are made in advance; answers stream as they are written; "Thinking"
  shows the moment you ask.
- **Degrade, never break.** If Ask AI is not allowed, the button simply is not there; with no signal,
  the words are still on screen.
- **Private by default.** Questions and photos go only to his own Claude account, after he allows it
  once; nothing is stored by the app.
- **Ship whole, ship tested**: every screen opened in a phone-sized browser, light and dark, before
  the link changes.

## Must have in v2

| Feature | What the visitor gets | How |
|---|---|---|
| **Ask the Guide** | A chat sheet on every stop: type a question, or take a photo and ask "what is this?". Answers in George's voice, short, about this stop, saying plainly when it is not sure | The page asks Claude on the owner's own account (the `sample` capability, with images). The prompt carries this stop's story, look-for items, facts and sources, so answers stay grounded. One-time consent; uses his normal Claude usage |
| **Photo questions** | Tap **Take a Photo**: the iPhone camera opens; snap; ask | A file input that opens the camera. A live camera feed is not available to these pages, so it is snap-then-ask |
| **Ask that does things** | "Take me to the T. rex", "play the bit about the jaw", "I will do this later" | Page functions Claude may call: open a stop, jump to a chapter, move a stop to later |
| **Suggested questions** | Three tappable starters per stop ("Is she real bone?", "How did she get in?") | Written with the stories |
| **Longer stories, in chapters** | About two hours of narration, each chapter titled | In progress: 4 researchers, a head researcher, copywriters, a fact-checker |
| **A photo for each thing to find** | A picture on each Look For item and chapter; the lock screen shows the chapter's photo | In progress: Commons photos, each credited |

## Next after v2

- **Spotted** tab: everything you found, and a postcard of the visit to share.
- **Family mode**: one question for children at each stop.
- **"I am lost"**: the map, the directions, and a photo question in one tap.
- **More languages**, if he wants them.

## Decided against, for now

- **A native iPhone app.** It would give a live camera feed, offline audio on the private version
  and Live Activities, but needs a Mac, Xcode and a paid Apple developer account; his rule is that
  nothing needs a Mac. Revisit only if First Walk becomes a product.
- **Reading AI answers aloud** with the phone's built-in voice: it is the robotic voice he ruled out.
  Answers are shown as text.
- **A public version with Ask AI**: asking Claude works only on the private link, signed in.
